# distinction

- **IPA / 音标:** /dɪˈstɪŋkʃn/
- **Pronunciation tip:** di-STINK-shn；重音在 STINK，-ction 发 /ʃn/
- **Etymology / 词源:** 拉丁 *distinguere*「separate / 区分」→ *distinction*
- **POS:** noun
- **Gloss (EN):** a clear difference that matters for meaning or policy
- **释义 (ZH):** 区分；关键差别
- **Register:** formal / neutral
- **Status:** new

- **Scene type:** meeting / copy review

## Collocations / 固定搭配（别直译）
- USE: *make a distinction between A and B*; *a key distinction*; *draw a distinction*
- AVOID: *make difference between*；*a big different*；别把「区分」拼成 *do distinction*

## Home scene (frozen)

You force the room to **make a distinction between** beta limits **and** GA rights so support doesn’t overpromise. **A key distinction**: beta users get early access, not SLA-backed uptime. You ask copy to split the FAQ into two bullets before Legal signs. In the thread you write: “If we blur this, Sev tickets become policy fights.”

## Why this chunk
- *make a distinction between A and B* = 划清必须分开的政策线；不是 *make difference between*。
- *a key distinction* = 点名那条关键差别，方便写进 FAQ/纪要。
- vs *nuance*：*nuance* 是细微色差；这里要的是硬界限 → *distinction*。

## Rewrite prompt

- 与 *nuance*（细微差别）相比，*distinction* 更强调「必须划清的界限」
