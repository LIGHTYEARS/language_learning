# steer

- **IPA / 音标:** /stɪə(r)/
- **Pronunciation tip:** steer（与 *stere* 无关）；过去式/分词 *steered*
- **Etymology / 词源:** 古英 *steoran*「掌舵、引导船只」→ 引申为引导讨论/项目方向
- **POS:** verb（名词用法少见；*steering committee* 是固定组织名）
- **Gloss (EN):** guide the direction; course-correct a discussion or initiative
- **释义 (ZH):** 引导；把讨论/项目拉回既定方向
- **Register:** neutral / formal（会议、评审、项目管理）
- **Status:** new
- **home_scene_id:** decision-criteria-drift
- **title cue:** `review · pixels again`
- **Scene type:** meeting / review
- **target chunks:** `steer … back to …`; `steer clear of …`
- **due:** 2026-09-18
- **stability:** 0
- **difficulty:** 5.0

## Collocations / 固定搭配（别直译）
- USE: *steer … back to …*; *steer … toward/away from*; *steer clear of …*; *steering committee*
- AVOID: *pull the topic return* / *guide direction to*；别字面「开方向盘」

## Home scene (frozen)
Design/planning review. The thread slides into button padding and a competitor’s marketing site. You interrupt once: “Can we **steer** this **back to** the decision criteria we wrote on the doc?” Two minutes later someone starts rumor-chat about headcount; you add, “Please **steer clear of** gossip — we still need an owner for the acceptance checks.” The room returns to the checklist.

## Cloze (same home scene)
Design/planning review drifts into pixels and competitor gossip. You: can we ______ this ______ the decision criteria? And please ______ gossip — we still need an owner for the acceptance checks.

## Why this chunk
- *steer … back to …* = 主动纠偏、拉回议程；比 *guide* 更有「偏了再扳」的力道。
- *steer clear of …* = 刻意避开（话题/风险）；不是 *avoid talk* 的直译堆砌。
- vs *drive*：*drive* 偏用力推进；这里要的是方向校正，不是加压。

## Rewrite prompt
弱句：「别扯皮了，回到决策标准，竞品八卦别聊。」  
→ 必须用 `steer … back to …` 和/或 `steer clear of …` 写一句会上能说的英文。

## Cue-fade (review)
1. CN：评审跑去抠像素/聊八卦 → 你拉回决策标准并让人避开八卦  
2. title cue only → 含 chunk 的一句  
3. 为什么不是 *guide* / *drive* / *avoid talk*  
4. 同场景写一句会议纪要（保留 chunk）  
5. transfer 仅在稳定后（如邮件里 *steer the initiative through Q4*）

## Pitfalls
- *steer clear of* 是短语，别拆成字面方向盘
- *steering committee* = 指导委员会
- 过去式 *steered*，不是 *stear*
